Project: District (by Zomato) automation

Problem Statement: 
1. Display all the sports activities available in your city for coming weekend,
2. get all movie languages,
3. capture login error by entering wrong login details.

Detailed Description:
----Activities----
1. Go to https://www.district.in/ and choose Mumbai location.
2. Go to Activities and apply filter of "This Weekend" & sort by "Price : Low to High"
3. Capture details of all activities and store it in suitable data structure in collection framework, And print the one having lowest price.
----Movies----
4. Go to movies and then get all languages available in Language Filter.
----Login----
5. Click on user profile and enter invalid mobile number and capture error and take screenshot.

Key Automation Scope:
Handling alerts, windows
Extract menu items from frames & store in collections
Scrolling down in web page
Filling simple form, Capture warning message
Navigation from Menus
Navigating back to home page
